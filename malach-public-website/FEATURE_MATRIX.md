# Malach Website Feature Matrix

## Presented as current Malach capabilities

- Google Ads intelligence and diagnostics
- Shopify economics and order-level contribution analysis
- Profit Center with revenue, cost, fee, shipping, spend, ROAS and POAS context
- Attribution by acquisition channel with reconciliation confidence
- Auto Agent with Live Canvas, Algorithm and Authority views
- Evidence, formula, threshold, decision and outcome ledger
- Opportunity intelligence for keywords, PMax creative and budget saturation
- Advantage competitor and search-market intelligence
- Malach Research Browser with source provenance
- Malach Chat grounded in verified business data
- Malach Voice with language, voice and selected-file context
- Evolution Studio for measured learning and owner-approved isolated improvements
- Multi-store Portfolio view for up to 20 stores
- Observe, Approval, Canary Auto and Full Auto operating modes
- Emergency control, action ledger, validation and rollback context
- System Map for provider and data-health visibility
- Knowledge-document support

## Clearly presented as coming soon

- Creative Intelligence Studio
- Cross-channel paid acquisition expansion
- Native mobile companion
- Public API and SDK
- Team roles and advanced approval policy
- Adaptive landing-page experiments

Coming-soon features must remain labeled until they are actually available in the production application.
