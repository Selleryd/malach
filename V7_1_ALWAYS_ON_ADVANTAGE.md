# Malach Public Website V7.1 — Always-On Advantage

V7.1 preserves the approved V7 visual system while refining buyer-facing language and product storytelling.

## Messaging changes
- Malach is positioned as a 24/7 intelligence and execution teammate.
- About page includes: “Malach means Angel and Messenger - working for you 24/7 with one goal. Making profitable decisions and actions.”
- Proprietary algorithms, statistics, probability, economics and evidence-based profit decisions are explained in consumer language.
- Intelligence explicitly covers continuous competitor, demand, market and performance monitoring.
- Auto Agent and Agent Canvas are explained as the operating layer that actually prepares and executes the work.
- Advantage is positioned as the combination of market intelligence and proprietary models that creates an unfair advantage.

## Experience changes
- Slightly smaller heading scale.
- New 24/7 operations timeline.
- New interactive proprietary-decision model.
- New interactive Agent Canvas simulation.
- New About “Angel and Messenger” brand story.
- Added subtle live motion without changing the approved V7 aesthetic.
